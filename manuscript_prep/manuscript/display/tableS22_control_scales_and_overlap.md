Scales and cohort membership are taken from the main text of the cited papers (extracts in `revision_sources/units_and_overlap_extracts.txt`); supplementary cohort lists were not read. "Up to" figures are upper limits computed from the sample sizes, not measured overlap. The GTEx file documentation describes `slope` only as a regression slope; that it refers to the alternative allele on normalized expression is the GTEx convention and was not confirmed from the file's own documentation. The *AGTR1* verdict does not depend on it, because the estimate is not distinguishable from zero. With overlapping samples, bias from weak instruments is toward the confounded observational association and scales with the degree of overlap and inversely with instrument strength; the F statistics of the two passing candidate controls were 2,305 and 115. Sensitivity result: with the *PCSK9* exposure taken from the European-ancestry discovery cohort (34,557; same variant) the estimate is 0.376 (95% CI, 0.367 to 0.385; F 1,723), against 0.387 with the combined cohort. Corrected 2026-10-11: the *PCSK9* and *ANGPTL3* rows previously named the discovery cohort (34,557), which had been recorded only because of a P-value tie; the combined-cohort row has the lower P value.
