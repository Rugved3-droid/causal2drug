A candidate is a variant at P<5×10⁻⁸ within the gene body ±100 kb in that tissue (recovery map; rules unchanged). 'no' means the gene was tested in the tissue and no variant qualified. The site of action is taken from the mechanism-of-action section of the regulatory label or product information named in the source column, read in October 2026; where that text does not name an organ, the note says so. Matching a site to GTEx tissues is our own step: vasculature to the three arteries, intestine to terminal ileum and colon, kidney to kidney cortex. Sample sizes are small and unequal (Table S20), so 'no' may reflect power.
Summary: 9 of 11 targets have a matching GTEx tissue; 1 of those have a candidate instrument in it (AGTR1).
