(X) X-linked gene.
Tiers describe candidate instrument availability, not validated instruments. CV-relevant or plasma-protein candidate: a qualifying variant in a cardiovascular-relevant tissue or a plasma cis-pQTL. Other-tissue candidate only: a qualifying variant only in other tissues. Locked: none in any checked resource; this is relative to the resources checked and is not a statement that no instrument exists.
Site of drug action is taken from the mechanism-of-action text of an FDA label or an EMA public assessment summary; it was not available for PLG. Regulatory status: FDA marketing status from Drugs@FDA (openFDA) and status in the EMA medicines table, both accessed October 2026; drug-by-drug records and sources are in Supplementary Table S18. 'Marketed' means at least one product with FDA status Prescription, or EMA status Authorised; nationally authorised European products are not in the EMA table.
† IL1B: the approved drug, rilonacept, binds both IL-1α and IL-1β (FDA label), and its approved cardiovascular indication in ChEMBL is pericarditis, whereas the source data label the target launched for atherosclerosis. IL1B is kept in this table and is not used as a named example elsewhere.
‡ APOB: the only linked drug, mipomersen (Kynamro), is listed as discontinued by the FDA and was refused marketing authorisation by the EMA in 2013. It was therefore approved at one time (by the FDA) and is no longer marketed; APOB remains a named example on that basis.
§ PLG: neither linked drug (anistreplase, streptokinase) has a record in Drugs@FDA or the EMA medicines table, so approval by either agency could not be confirmed from these sources. PLG is kept in this table and is not used as a named example elsewhere.
